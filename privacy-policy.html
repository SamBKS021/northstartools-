Privacy Policy — NorthStarTools Manager

Last updated: November 16, 2026

NorthStarTools Manager is a private tool used by the owner of the NorthStarTools Pinterest business account to manage that account's own boards and pins through the official Pinterest API.

What data the app accesses

The app accesses only the owner's own Pinterest account, using the scopes boards:read, boards:write, pins:read, pins:write and user_accounts:read. It reads the account's boards and pins and creates boards and pins on the owner's behalf.

What data is stored

The app stores the owner's Pinterest OAuth access and refresh tokens on the owner's own server, solely to keep the connection working. No passwords are stored. No data about any other person is collected.

What data is shared

Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. The only data sent to Pinterest is the content the owner chooses to publish (pins, boards, titles, descriptions and links).
